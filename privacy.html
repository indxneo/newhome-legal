Privacy Policy

Soft Spot for iPhone · Effective October 26, 2026

The short version: Soft Spot does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game itself does not connect to the internet; its one optional purchase goes through Apple's App Store. Everything the game saves stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

Soft Spot is made by indxneo, an independent developer. If you have a question about this policy, email alishersv.dev@outlook.com.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any information about you or how you use the game. There is no sign-in, no advertising and no third-party analytics.

The full game (in-app purchase)

Chapter 1 is free. One optional in-app purchase opens the rest of the game. Apple handles the payment with your Apple Account; we never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. On your iPhone, Apple only tells the game whether the full game has been bought, so it can open it, also after you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone. Purchases are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What the game keeps on your iPhone

To remember your progress, the game stores a few small files on your device:

  • your finished client jobs, their stars and how long each one took;
  • the rooms and furniture you set up, including Free mode;
  • your settings: music and the song you picked, sound effects, haptics, reduce motion and language;
  • whether the full game is open on this iPhone.

These files never leave your device through the game. They are removed when you delete the app. Like any app data, they may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Apple

The game is distributed through Apple's App Store and TestFlight. If you have chosen to share analytics or crash reports with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage statistics. We use them only to fix problems. This is handled by Apple, under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

If you email us

If you write to support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to answer you and delete it when it is no longer needed. We never add you to a mailing list.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Что игра хранит на вашем iPhone

Чтобы помнить ваш прогресс, игра хранит на устройстве несколько небольших файлов:

  • выполненные заказы, звёзды за них и сколько времени ушло на каждый;
  • комнаты и мебель, которые вы обустроили, в том числе в свободном режиме;
  • настройки: музыка и выбранная песня, звуки, вибрация, «Меньше анимации» и язык;
  • открыта ли на этом iPhone полная версия.

Игра никуда не отправляет эти файлы. Они удаляются вместе с приложением. Как и данные любого приложения, они могут попасть в ваши резервные копии в iCloud или на компьютере, а за них отвечает Apple по своей политике конфиденциальности.
